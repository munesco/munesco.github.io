Privacy Policy – Munesco PinSync

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Munesco PinSync is an internal tool used only by Munesco, the owner of the Etsy shops MunescoDesign, MunescoGift and MunescoKids. It creates Pins from our own Etsy listings and publishes them to our own Pinterest business accounts.

Information we access. When our Pinterest accounts authorize the app, it accesses basic account information, boards, Pins and Pin analytics through the official Pinterest API. It only accesses accounts owned by Munesco.

How we use it. The data is used only to create and schedule Pins and to review the performance of our own Pins.

Storage and sharing. Access tokens and data are stored locally on our own computer. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not collect data from other Pinterest users.

Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest under Settings → Apps / Third-party access.
